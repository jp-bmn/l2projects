# Welcome to the Repo

This repository is a collection of independent projects, each living in its own branch. Browse the branches to explore — below is a quick rundown of what's here and what each one does.

---

## 🎬 Prime Video Mockup (Dark)
**Branch:** `prime-video-mockup`

Ever look at Prime Video and think *"this could feel so much better"?* This is that thought, fully built out. It's a single-file interactive prototype of a redesigned Prime Video experience — smoother carousels, better keyboard navigation, and a slick "Deep Dive" feature that generates quick mood-matching write-ups for whatever you're about to watch. No installs, no dependencies. Just open it and poke around.

---

## 🎮 Game Rack
**Branch:** `game-rack`

Your gaming backlog is a mess. Game Rack fixes that. Answer a few questions about what you're in the mood for, and it builds you a smart, balanced play list based on your ratings, how long each game takes, and how far you actually want to get. Can't decide what to play *right now*? Game Rack will pick something for you — whether it's in your library or not. The final boss is indecision. Game Rack defeats it.

---

## 📬 Open Courier
**Branch:** `open-courier`

Think Twilio, but with two of its most annoying gaps filled in. Open Courier is a developer-focused messaging platform that handles the core sending you'd expect, plus a **Pull Inbox** (so you can receive messages without setting up webhooks) and a **Billing Intelligence Dashboard** (so you're never caught off guard by a bill you didn't see coming). Built for developers who want control, not surprises.

---

## 🎤 GitHub Desktop Assistant
**Branch:** `github-desktop-assistant`

GitHub Desktop, but now it talks back. This AI-powered tool adds voice control to GitHub Desktop — speak your commands instead of clicking around, and GitHub will respond out loud to let you know what it just did. Commit, push, pull, branch — all hands-free. Great for when your hands are busy, or you just really want your workflow to feel like a sci-fi movie.

---

## 🌿 GreenEyes NYC Reduction Monitor
**Branch:** `greeneyes-nyc`

New York City's Local Law 97 means buildings now face real fines for excess carbon emissions — and most owners have no idea where they stand. GreenEyes is a monitoring tool that tracks energy usage and LL97 compliance in real time, flags buildings that are drifting toward penalties, and sends automated alerts before it becomes a costly problem. Think of it as a carbon budget tracker that actually pays for itself.
